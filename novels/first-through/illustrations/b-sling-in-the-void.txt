SCENE. Sling, in the gallery void at the waist of the Nine. A rectangular
well of open air forty metres across cut through the middle of the station,
six levels of walkway and handrail stacked above and below it, the noise of
the anchor hall coming up from underneath. The game is banned four times over
and played anyway. Only about 20 kids are watching sparsely distributed over the rows of the gallery. 
Some are lounging about watching, some are very animated.   

THE ACTION. A girl of fifteen — Lint, wiry, strong through the shoulders and
forearms, a swimmer's build, dark hair cut short enough to stop mattering —
has just thrown a weighted canvas bag flat and low across the void. Catch her
at the end of the throw, feet still behind the painted plate on the deck
edge, body following the arm, face doing nothing at all. The bag is small and
already a long way out.

THE SUIT. Close-fitting matte cloth, long-sleeved and long-legged, about the
weight of a good shirt — plain soft working clothes, not shiny, not
translucent, no seams and no fastenings. Dyed a deep green, with one small
plain Threshold flash at the left shoulder. The dye is BLEACHED OUT in faint
pale cloudy patches across the shoulders and all the way up both forearms.
That bleaching is thirty-four crossings and it is the most important detail in
the frame. On her feet, two mismatched boots from two different stations.

THE OTHERS. Across the gap, a boy of nineteen on the far rail about to catch,
annoyed. Along the galleries above and below, forty-odd kids hanging over
handrails watching, and one small child sitting on the very top rail with a
flask, not moving. Somewhere in the air, hand-sized iridescent creatures lit
bright, flying the gap.

DETAIL. A stanchion by the plate, scratched over two centuries with a tally of
names and marks.

LIGHT. Warm working lamps on every gantry, falling away into the dark middle
of the void. The subject is lit from the side and below by deck lighting.

STYLE. Sports reportage at an amateur game in a bad venue. Mid-distance,
slight motion blur in the thrown arm only.

AVOID. Zero gravity poses. Anyone floating. Glowing effects on the bag.
Heroic low angles. A cheering crowd. Making the children look plucky.
