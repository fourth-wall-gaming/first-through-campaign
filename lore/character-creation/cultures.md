**Distribute 100 points** among your culture's Standard skills, and choose **up to
three** Professional skills from its list and spend points on those too. No more
than 15 points into any single skill.

Where you grew up is most of who you are out here, and the lists are the proof.

## The Garden — born on the Remainder

Earth. Weather nobody made, an ocean you can walk into, six thousand years of
farmland, and no money at all. Outdoors is normal to you and strange to everyone
you will ever work with.

**Standard:** Athletics, Endurance, **Swim**, Locale, Perception, Customs,
Willpower, Brawn

**Professional:** Craft (any), Healing, Lore (the Remainder), Navigation,
Survival, Track

*Nobody else in the Causeway has Swim. They will ask you to prove it and then not
believe you.*

## Station-born — the Nine, Procyon, Alpha Centauri

Raised in a turning cylinder in a port. You have never stood on a planet. You
can read a queue, a hall and a stranger's boots at ten paces.

**Standard:** Customs, Influence, Insight, Vacuum, Evade, Deceit, Perception,
Sleight

**Professional:** Commerce, Courtesy, Streetwise, Lore (a system), Acting,
Mechanisms

## Holding-born — Coldwater, Teegarden's, the farm worlds

A settlement small enough that everybody has a job and everybody knows yours.
Grain under lamps or a red sun, and a council that meets in public.

**Standard:** Endurance, Brawn, Locale, First Aid, Willpower, Athletics, Drive,
Customs

**Professional:** Craft (any), Healing, Survival, Commerce, Lore (a system),
Track

## Shelter-born — Tau Ceti, YZ Ceti

Underground, because something comes down every few weeks. Vaulted halls lit from
shafts, built by people for whom a roof is a theological matter. You were raised
careful and quiet and you know where the strong walls are.

**Standard:** Perception, Willpower, Stealth, Insight, Locale, Endurance, Sing,
Customs

**Professional:** Engineering, Lore (any), Musicianship, Science (any), Craft
(any), Navigation

*YZ Ceti children grow up with a sky that is audible. Their Musicianship is the
best in human space and they are unbearable about it.*

## Works-born — Barnard's, the Gliese 65 refineries

Three shifts, forever, and the air tastes of it. Your vote is your shift. You
were useful at eleven.

**Standard:** Brawn, Endurance, Vacuum, Athletics, Drive, Willpower, Unarmed,
Customs

**Professional:** Engineering, Mechanisms, Craft (any), Commerce, Lore (Tally
procedure), Survival

## Belt-born — Epsilon Eridani, the Luhman 16 decks

Vacuum, rocks and drift, or a sky with no ground in it. Everything either floats
or falls and you learned which very early.

**Standard:** Vacuum, Evade, Perception, Athletics, Stealth, Conceal, Endurance,
Customs

**Professional:** Pilot, Navigation, Survival, Mechanisms, Streetwise, Science
(any)
