**Distribute 100 points** among your career's Standard skills, and choose **up to
three** Professional skills from its list. No more than 15 into any one skill.

**Every one of these includes Crossing.** A character with Crossing is a
first-through; a character without it is somebody else's story.

Your knack suggests a career and does not dictate one. A Quick who works as a
Doorwright is a slightly odd and very employable person.

## Courier

The standard, and three-quarters of the service. Carrying books, carrying news,
carrying whatever a hall hands you, across as many doors as the rota says.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, Customs, Influence, Perception, Evade, Insight,
Deceit, Athletics

**Professional:** Commerce, Streetwise, **Reading**, Courtesy, Lore (a system),
Acting

## Pathfinder

First through a door that has moved, to find out where it goes now. The job the
service is named for, and the one with the funerals.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, Perception, Vacuum, Endurance, Evade, Athletics,
Willpower, Stealth

**Professional:** Survival, Navigation, Lore (anchors), Science (any), Track,
Pilot

## Doorwright

Housings, collars and load beds. A first-through who can read a door is worth
three who cannot, and every hall in human space would like one.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, Perception, Brawn, Vacuum, Willpower, Endurance,
Locale, Athletics

**Professional:** Mechanisms, Engineering, Lore (anchors), Craft (pressure
fitting), Science (physics), Lockpicking

## Lifeline

The ones sent when a hall calls for help. Evacuations, pressure casualties, and
the carries nobody else will take.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, First Aid, Endurance, Athletics, Willpower,
Perception, Brawn, Vacuum

**Professional:** Healing, Survival, Craft (any), Science (biology), Lore (a
people), Track

## Envoy

Carries people rather than books: negotiators, specialists, prisoners, the old.
The most political job a fifteen-year-old can hold and the one with the best
clothes.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, Influence, Insight, Customs, Willpower, Deceit,
Perception, Dance

**Professional:** Courtesy, Oratory, Commerce, Lore (Tally procedure), Acting,
Musicianship

## Scout-runner

Speed as a profession. The ones sent when the answer has to be somewhere before
anybody else's answer is.

**Standard:** **Crossing**, Athletics, Evade, Endurance, Stealth, Perception,
Vacuum, Unarmed

**Professional:** Acrobatics, Navigation, Survival, Streetwise, Track, Lore (a
system)
