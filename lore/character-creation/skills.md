### The two swaps

**Vacuum** is moving and working outside: suit discipline, handholds, tethers,
microgravity, and not dying of a torn glove. Every station child has some.

**Crossing** is the discipline of the door itself. Not the knack and not the
fraying — the *craft*: how steady you are in the housing, how you hold it open,
how you carry somebody who is frightened, how long you can keep going and how
honestly you judge when to stop.

It is the one skill every first-through in human space has, and it is the one
nobody else has at all. **A character with Crossing is a first-through.** That is
what the word means.
