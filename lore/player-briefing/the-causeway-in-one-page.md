Nine hundred and seventy-five years ago, people who were richer than us threw
thousands of robot ships at the sky. Each one carried half of a door. Where a
ship arrived and woke up, the half it carried switched on, and from that moment
you could step between two stars as easily as between two rooms.

Nobody has worked out how to build one since. We use theirs.

**Inside a system, it is the future.** Every star the doors reach holds a full
civilisation of millions or billions of people: fusion torches, arcologies, real
medicine, real industry, real navies, and a **mind** that runs the whole system
and knows everything in it, all at once, to anyone who asks. Almost nothing is
written down, because almost nothing needs to be. You ask the air and are told.

**Across a door, there is no signal of any kind.** No radio, no message, no
mind. A mind cannot cross. Nothing manufactured crosses, and no information
crosses except in a living thing, and the only living things that can cross are
children. That is the whole channel between stars: one fifteen-year-old at a
time, carrying a friend (a fetch) who remembers for a living.

So step through a door and you are there, instantly, however far it is. Go the
other way, with a ship, or a shout, and you are back to the speed of light, which
is to say decades. The map of the inhabited galaxy is not a space. It is a
**graph**: a set of places joined by the doors somebody happened to build, and
nothing else.

Two hundred light years away can be one step. The star next door, with no door
in it, may as well not exist.

**This has one consequence that runs everything.** Since nothing beats a person
walking, news travels exactly as fast as the child carrying it. Thirty billion
people, and the pipe that joins them is a drinking straw. There is no calling
ahead. A thing that happened on the far side of a door is not a fact anybody has
until somebody walks back through and says so, which is why people who walk
through doors for a living are worth more than anyone else in human space, and
why the job you have just taken exists.

**The doors are old and some of them are dying.** When one dies it does not
explode. It simply stops, and everyone on the far side is still there, alive,
and now unreachable forever. That is what the Threshold is for, and it is what
you will spend your career trying to stop.

**And one door has not opened yet.** It is due. Everybody knows the date.
