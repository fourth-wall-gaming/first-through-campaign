**Open thread. v1 must not foreclose it.**

## What is true

A Bell sixlimb has a brain the size of a plum and acquires real syntax -- tense,
argument, jokes that are recognisably jokes. Nothing in the terminator band of a
tidally-locked world selects for that. There is no prey that requires grammar.

The Threshold's xenobiology note is two pages and the second page is questions.

Somebody built them.

## The open decision

There are two threads running toward the same place, and **v1 deliberately does
not decide whether they meet:**

1. **Tolk's species was engineered** by someone with a very long reach.
2. **One door in the network has no seedship** -- no launch record, nothing --
   which means somebody paired anchors before the First Reach.

**These may be the same revelation or two different ones.** Both readings are
live and both are good:

- **Same.** Whoever built the anchors also built a species that could talk, and
  Tolk is a message left where it would eventually be found. Tidier, and it
  makes the companion the key to the whole arc.
- **Different.** The anchor-builders are a cold absence with no interest in us,
  and Tolk's makers are somebody else entirely -- possibly much more recent,
  possibly still around. Messier, and it keeps Act 5's absence genuinely empty,
  which is the more frightening option.

This is recorded as an **authoring decision for a later release**, not an
oversight. Spec section 16 names it as open and the author has not called it.

## What v1 is allowed to do

Keep the thread live and answer nothing.

- Tolk carries the passion *somebody made me and I would like to know who* at 45.
  It is on the sheet from session one.
- The agenda **Find out what Bell was** exists, is active, and advances from odd
  angles -- mostly Tolk asking an unexpected question in the middle of somebody
  else's scene. The beat at the Yards is the model.
- The fact *Tolk's species did not evolve its language* sits in the graph as
  **not-yet-true**, so nobody can establish it and the engine will not let a GM
  accidentally treat it as known.

## What v1 must not do

- Do not have anybody speculate aloud about who built them. Not Calloway, not an
  instructor, not Tolk.
- Do not connect it to the Quorum. The Quorum is a man with a method; this is
  older.
- Do not make Tolk sad about it. Tolk is **curious**, which is harder to write
  and much better: a person looking for an author, cheerfully, at their own pace.
