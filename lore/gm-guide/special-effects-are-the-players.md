Short, and absolute.

**When the player wins an exchange, the GM never chooses the special effect.**
You name what is available in that situation and the player picks.

That is `TABLE.md` §7, and in this campaign it is the single biggest difference
between a fight a twelve-year-old enjoys and a fight they endure. A special
effect is the moment the dice stop happening *to* them and start being a
decision they made. Take that away and combat is a slot machine with extra
steps.

## How it goes at the table

1. The exchange resolves. The player has earned one effect, or two.
2. You say what the situation offers — by name, in plain words, three or four at
   most. *You can put him on the floor. You can get the baton off him. You can
   put yourself between him and the crawl. You can give him a dead arm and end
   this now.*
3. They choose. You narrate what that looks like.

Keep the list short and situational. A list of eleven options is a rulebook; a
list of three that are all obviously about *this* corridor is a choice.

## In this campaign specifically

The non-lethal rule and the special-effect list agree with each other, and you
should lean on that. The effects this campaign wants in front of a player are
the ones that end a fight without ending a person:

- **Trip**, **Grip**, **Disarm**, **Take Weapon** — the whole Tally repertoire,
  and the whole first-through repertoire too
- **Stun Location** — a dead arm, a leg that will not hold
- **Withdraw** — getting out, which is a win and should be offered as one
- **Bypass Armour** against AP 4 grey plate, which is otherwise miserable

Offer **Impale** and **Maximise Damage** only when a weapon in play could
plausibly do that, which in this campaign is rare and should feel like it.

## The one thing not to do

Do not offer an effect that would make the fight lethal, and then rule it
non-lethal after the player picks it. That teaches them their choices are
decorative, which is worse than any amount of danger. If an effect is not on
the table in this campaign, it is not on the list.
