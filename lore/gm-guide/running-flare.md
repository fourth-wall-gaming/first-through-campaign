## The clock, concretely

**Forty minutes of station time from arrival to the flare.** Keep it on paper,
never in prose.

| Marker | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| ~30 left | Deck lighting browns out about every ninety seconds |
| ~20 left | The hull ticks. Nobody in the room mentions it |
| ~12 left | Radio hiss rises until the crew stop using the channel |
| ~5 left | Somebody has opened the shutter on the upper processing deck |
| 0 | Hard white through the shutter. Everybody behind mass, or not |

If the player is still fighting at zero, do not kill anybody. The flare hits,
the lights go white, the corridor fills with people who have stopped caring
about the barricade, and the scene resolves in burns, concussion and a shared
emergency. That is a worse outcome than winning and nobody has to die for it to
land.

## The four-way solution matrix

| Beat | The Runner | The Tinker | The Talker | The Fieldhand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reach the anchor side | Takes the maintenance crawl | Gels the hinge side, reroutes via the ore hoist | Talks the crew off the stack | Moves the injured first, which clears it |
| Handle the tallymen | Fights them, and can | Drops the stack between them | Reads the Tally's own hazard clause to Brace | Treats one of them, which changes the room |
| Handle the crew | Carries two of them | Brings the stack down controlled | Gives them a reason that is not surrender | Takes away the fear the barricade was built out of |
| Beat the clock | Speed | A shortcut | Ending it early | Fewer casualties to move |

## The Talker's route, in full

It is the cleverest ending and it must actually work. The Tally's own procedure
voids an impound during a **declared hazard**, and a flare count is a declared
hazard. [[Tallyman Captain Brace]] has Lore (Tally Procedure) 88 and will know
this is true the moment it is said out loud.

He will honour it. He is not a bully — he is a man whose ledger has to balance,
and a voided impound balances. Let this land without a roll if the player finds
the argument themselves; make it an Influence call against his Willpower only if
they have it roughly right but cannot name the clause.

## Three waves, and permission to use fewer

Two refinery hands, then four tallymen, then Brace. **Use as many as the session
wants and no more.** If the player is enjoying the fight, run all three. If
they found the crawl in the first five minutes, let that be the answer and give
them the flare for a climax instead. The waves are a budget, not a sequence.

## What not to spend

Brace says *the Quorum* once, as a signature, and admits the timing of the
impound order once, with unease, and then stops. He does not speculate. He does
not know. No second clue this sitting.
