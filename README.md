# First Through

**A Mythras Imperative campaign for mythras-gm — a first roleplaying game for
10-15 year olds, set in the real stellar neighbourhood.**

Nine hundred and seventy-five years ago an earlier human expansion launched
robotic seedships at a twentieth of lightspeed, each carrying one half of an
anchor pair. Where a ship arrived and woke, a door opened. That is the only way
between stars. Transit is instantaneous; everything else — including radio —
obeys the speed of light.

So the inhabited galaxy is a **graph, not a volume**. Nothing outruns a person,
news travels at the speed of a courier walking through a door, and the moment
you step through, nobody can reach you.

You are a **first-through**: fifteen, newest and youngest the Threshold has
taken in years. Your handler cannot follow you. Your companion can.

## The astronomy is real

Every system, spectral type, planet, distance and oddity in this campaign is a
real and current fact, cited in the lore. We invent only what people built
there. The map is Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star, the brown dwarfs of
Luhman 16, the water-ice clouds of WISE 0855, the twin flare stars of
Gliese 65, the habitable-zone super-Earths at GJ 887 and Gl 725, the radio
aurorae of YZ Ceti, and van Maanen's Star — a white dwarf visibly consuming the
wreckage of its own planets.

Transit time is distance in light years times twenty, which puts the frontier
at 48.75 light years. That is **LHS 1140**, the best known rocky
habitable-zone planet outside the solar system. Its door opens in about a year,
and it is the spine of the campaign.
