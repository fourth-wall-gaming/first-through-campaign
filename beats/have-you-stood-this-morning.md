The player's first standing: off the plate, to the rail, say out loud where you
went. There is nothing there to look at and that is the point -- people from
places with records keep hunting for the object.

Somebody -- a hall child, ideally, who cannot cross and knows more about it than
anybody -- explains it, and is proud to, and teaches them to hear all four parts
of a line instead of the first two. And mentions that if you ask the Old Man
about Kapteyn's he gives you four words in a dead woman's voice, and has for
three and a half centuries.
