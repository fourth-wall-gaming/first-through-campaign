The Tally's procedure voids an impound during a declared hazard. He knows it.
He has known it since the count started climbing and he has been not-saying it
for a shift and a half.

If a PC says it to him, he stops, and something goes out of his face, and he
honours it. If nobody does, he does it himself at the last possible moment and
never mentions that he nearly did not.
