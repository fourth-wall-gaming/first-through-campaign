The cheapest way into the whole act, available to any character who will talk to
a stranger, and it costs the player nothing but a question.

Two Wolf Gate loaders go past with crates, mid-grumble: *it's sticky again, I
stood there like an idiot.* Follow them and they are delighted to explain, at
length, because they are loaders with a grievance and somebody is actually
asking.

## What they give, for free

- **What sticky is.** You step up, you give it your weight, and nothing. Not a
  push-back — it goes solid, like the ring is a wall. You come off, give it a
  second, go again, and it has you.
- **The argument about the word.** Set is what happens once. Sticky is when it
  is doing it all week. *Set's your fault and sticky's the door's.* They will
  disagree about this the whole way down the stair and never resolve it.
- **The wrong trick.** *Don't brace against it.* One of them insists this is the
  only trick and the other insists bracing has nothing to do with it, and
  neither of them knows, and it does not matter.

## And the sentence that closes the door on it

> *Seven's sticky every spring. Has been since I started. Come summer it stops,
> and nobody thinks about it.*

He is describing a thing that happens every spring and he has twelve springs of evidence. **Let the player
be reassured.** If they roll to catch what is actually in that sentence, let the
roll decide honestly — a failure here is not a punishment, it is a competent
adult being convincing about something he has every reason to believe.

The thing nobody in that stairwell can know is that the twins reach closest
approach once in twenty-six and a half years, which is longer than any of them
has worked that door.

## Running it

Ninety seconds, then let them go. Both loaders are good-humoured, neither is
ominous, and the GM must not put a significant pause anywhere in it.

The player should walk away from this beat **feeling better** about a door they
were not worried about. That is the correct outcome and it is what makes
[[Pell Ivor's board]] land when it comes.

## The one that matters later

The older loader is on the stair again at dawn the next day, with a crate on his
shoulder and a queue of twenty people going nowhere behind him, and all he says
is *sticky*, and goes back to waiting.

Same man, same word, and by then the player knows what it means. Play it exactly
as flat as the first time.
