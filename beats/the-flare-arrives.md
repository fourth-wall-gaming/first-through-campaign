Hard white through the shutter. The lighting does not brown out, it simply
stops, and the hull noise becomes something people describe differently
afterwards.

If the barricade is still up when this fires, nobody dies -- but the corridor
fills with people who have stopped caring about it, and the scene resolves in
burns and concussion and a shared emergency. That is a worse ending than winning
and it does not require anybody's death to land.
