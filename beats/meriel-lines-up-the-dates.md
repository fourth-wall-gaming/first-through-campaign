She has suspected for two years. Tonight she puts the dates beside each other.

She does not tell Sep. She says it aloud to the room, once, quietly, with the
room's mind listening: four findings, four slipped quarters. It is attested the
moment she says it, and anyone who asks the room can hear it in her voice. She
will not be able to take it back, and a visitor could find it.
