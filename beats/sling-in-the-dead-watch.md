Banned four times. Played anyway, in the hours when the hall is quietest, at the
waist of the cylinder where a committed fifteen-year-old can cross the open space
in one jump.

Ferren is good at it and pleasant about being good at it. A fathom is better and
very far from pleasant.

If the player wants to play, let them, and let it cost nothing.
