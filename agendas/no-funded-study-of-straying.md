Nothing is being suppressed. Hale would say so and believe it.

What happens is that studies are not funded, courier slots for a records clerk
at [[van Maanen's Star]] are allocated elsewhere, and a proposal dies in a
committee for four separate ordinary reasons. One meeting at a time, for about
eleven years.

Because a berth whose door might move is worth a fraction of a berth whose door
might not, and four generations of his family are in that berth.

Advances every time something quietly does not happen. **Thwarted by publicity**
-- by making him say out loud, in a room with other people in it, what he is
actually arguing for.
