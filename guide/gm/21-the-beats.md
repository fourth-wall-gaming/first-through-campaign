# The Beats

A beat's clock entry is the **latest** it can happen. Conditions accelerate;
time only backstops.

<!-- timeline -->

## What everybody is trying to do

An agenda is a thing a person wants badly enough to spend something on, with a
clock on it. When the clock fills, they get it — unless somebody is in the way.

<!-- agendas -->

## Running them

Three habits, and they are the difference between this act working and this act
being a lecture.

**Let the beats fire out of order.** The list above is what happens if nobody
interferes, and somebody is about to interfere.

**Never narrate a beat the player is not in.** If an agenda advances offscreen,
the player meets the *consequence* — a queue that has got longer, a question
nobody asked, somebody who has stopped being where they were.

**One talking-in-a-room scene, then cut.** The moment a beat becomes four
adults agreeing about procedure, something physical happens instead. Paper is
the key to a door. It is never the door.
