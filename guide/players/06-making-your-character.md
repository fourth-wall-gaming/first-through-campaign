# Making Your Own First-Through

There are no ready-made characters in this game. You build your own, out loud,
at the table, in about twenty minutes, and defining your own person is most of
the point of a first roleplaying game.

It goes in three stages and you spend points in each one. **No more than
fifteen points into any single skill in any one stage** — which is the rule
that stops you building somebody who can only do one thing.

| Stage | Points | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Culture | 100 | Where you grew up |
| Career | 100 | What the Threshold trained you as |
| Bonus | 150 | Who you actually are |

Table: The Three Stages

Then three **passions** — the things you care about enough to argue with an
adult about — and a fetch, who is a person and gets made like one.

And say what colour your weave is. It is not a statistic and it costs nothing,
and it is the first thing anybody in a hall will see about you. It came out of
the vat off-white; what you did about that is up to you.

## Where you grew up

Six upbringings. The lists are the argument: a Garden child has **Swim**, and
nobody else in the Causeway does, and they will ask you to prove it and then
not believe you.
