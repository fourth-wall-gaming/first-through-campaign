# The Neighbourhood

Every star in this book is a real star and every figure is a real measurement.
The distances, the colours, the planets and the oddities are what astronomers
have actually found. Only the people and what they built are invented.

Here is what your character already knows, the way a courier knows it: by which
door it is and what it smells like when you come out.

## The frontier

The ships are still flying. Twenty years per light year, launched nine hundred
and seventy-five years ago, which puts the arriving edge at a little under
forty-nine light years out and moving one light year every twenty years.

Next year it reaches **LHS 1140** — and that one matters, because LHS 1140 b is
the best rocky planet in the habitable zone of anything anybody has ever found.
Every door that has ever opened has opened onto rock, vacuum, cloud or
somebody's hull. That one will open onto weather.

Its ship has been flying for nine hundred and seventy-six years. Do the
subtraction.
