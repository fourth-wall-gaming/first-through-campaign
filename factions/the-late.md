**Player-visible.** This is what a hall, a tallyman or a first-through can see
and say about them. The GM's file is [[The Late]], and what lies underneath it
stays there.

**Standing toward the player:** the same school, the same Yards, a few years on
and gone the other way. A first-through looks at a Late and knows exactly what
they are looking at, which is the worst of it.

**How you know one:** an absence. Every first-through carries a plain flash at the
left shoulder of the weave. The Late cut theirs out, and what is left is a scar
in the cloth. They carry no lading either. A courier with nothing to deliver
is in a hall for something else.

**How they come:** one at a time, because a door passes one child with her fetch
beside her. A trickle of very good fighters into a hall built for nothing of the
kind. They hit and go back through; one who stays does not last. Nobody who is
landlocked can follow, which means the only answer to them is a first-through.

**How to play them:** at full weight. They carry living blades and grown plate,
undyed weave, and the quiet of people who have already done the sum. A hall
that has seen a Threshold team knows what a Threshold team looks like, and this
is not that. Name the danger in the fiction before a player commits to it.

**Field face:** [[Ines Caul]], twenty-two, who is past it and still crossing.

**What they want:** what they were promised, which is that there is something
afterwards. None of them can tell you what it is.
